## NQ-103

The customer requested the return on January 31. The February 7 refund brings it into this reporting month. Its 21-day deadline fell on January 31, so a refund-date comparison produces a false alarm.

**2025-A#standard**

Request a return within 21 calendar days after delivery. Requests on the deadline qualify.

**2025-A#holiday**

No holiday extension applies in this edition.
