# North Quay Supply · 2024 edition

## standard
Request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. Requests on the deadline qualify.

## holiday
For orders purchased from 2024-11-15 through 2024-12-24, extend the deadline to 2025-01-31 if later.

## exception
For custom items, a supervisor sets the return window case by case.

## payment
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Posting time depends on the bank.
